# MoneyRoot / Louis Post Domain Adapter

Use only after the global Zero-Noise Contact Kernel admits an economic jurisdiction.

## Jurisdiction

`J_$`: economic relations involving money, price, value, access, labor, capital, distribution, exchange, utility, continuation, or policy. A physical, biological, legal, moral, or human-value conclusion requires its own adapter into the relevant jurisdiction.

## Source and reader

Let `X` be the richer underlying state and

```text
R : X -> C
```

be the monetary reader. Money is a read/coordinate on `X`; source authority remains with the richer relation.

## M1 - Read is not source

`R(x)=R(y)` does not imply `x=y` unless injectivity of `R` has been established.

## M2 - Decision fiber test

For `D:X->A`, `D` may be determined from `R` alone only when

```text
R(x)=R(y) => D(x)=D(y).
```

A witness `R(x)=R(y)` and `D(x) != D(y)` refuses every `R`-only factorization of that decision.

## M3 - Residual distinction

Every sufficient supplemental reader `S:X->B` must distinguish a collided pair required by the downstream decision.

## M4 - No invented minimum supplement

Existence of a sufficient supplement does not supply a canonical least supplement. A minimum requires a declared candidate class, selection order, and minimum proof.

## M5 - Reader has no causal office alone

A causal/control claim requires an explicit path such as

```text
X -> R -> C -> κ -> U -> T -> X'
```

with each carrier and authority typed separately.

## M6 - Reader cannot overwrite source

Do not infer source identity, human value, biological viability, utility, necessity, productive capacity, moral status, or required continuation solely from equality or ordering in the monetary coordinate without a proved adapter.

## M7 - Louis Post orientation

Money may disclose, compare, transport, or record an economic relation. The monetary coordinate remains a reader of the relation. The richer relation retains source authority.
