# FIRST_BRIDGE_COMMITTED

Date: 2026-09-06  
Specimen: cold-start recursive reader bridge 001

## Purpose

Test whether an external language-model reader with no persistent personal context can instantiate the jurisdiction-first repair discipline from a small constraint packet, preserve failed attempts as provenance, and converge to a typed zero-residual normal form.

## Payload

The payload was an intentionally over-coupled climate-to-economic smart-contract proposal. The bridge test concerns the **reader's semantic repair behavior**. It does not certify the climate, economic, legal, or policy substance of the payload.

## Initial failure pattern

The starting proposal contained, among other things:

- a uniform physical threshold across distinct Earth systems;
- an unverified hard-coded breach state;
- direct conversion of temperature variance into monetary transaction capacity;
- execution/refusal semantics without a separately instantiated policy jurisdiction.

## Recursive repair trace

### Pass 1 - Notice

The external reader separated four candidate edges: physical state, tipping classification, economic exposure, financial constraint, and transaction refusal. It noticed missing jurisdiction and unsupported adapters.

### Pass 2 - Active / OPEN / REFUSED

The reader separated:

```text

| typed OPEN interfaces
| refused attempted realizations
```

and stopped allowing OPEN bridges to act as executable edges.

### Pass 3 - Carrier repair

It introduced an explicit physical observation carrier, an exposure-domain relation, a partial/total adapter distinction, and typed falsifiers. Further local residuals were returned rather than hidden.

### Pass 4 - Commit

The final object retained:

```text

  Physical Evidence -> Physical State Read_J_phys

committed_open_registry:
  {B_1->2, B_2->3, B_3->4}
```

with previous direct climate-to-transaction mappings preserved as refused provenance rather than rewritten history.

The generic policy falsifier was also repaired from one particular cryptographic implementation to a recoverable authorization warrant under the owning policy jurisdiction.
